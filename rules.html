📜 Server Rules

Welcome to Abyssinian Craft SMP. To keep our community friendly, safe, and enjoyable for everyone, all members must follow the rules below.

1. No Doxing

Sharing personal information about anyone is strictly prohibited.

2. Respect Staff

Do not disrespect staff members or ignore their instructions.

3. No Botting or Destructive Behavior

Using bots, exploits, or destructive actions is not allowed.

4. Follow Discord TOS

All members must follow Discord Terms of Service and Guidelines.

5. No Excessive Pinging

Avoid repeatedly mentioning or spamming members.

6. No NSFW Content

Posting NSFW media may result in an instant ban.

7. No Advertising

Do not advertise servers, websites, projects, or services.

8. No Staff Harassment

Excessive pings or harassment towards staff will result in punishment.

9. No Toxicity or Threats

Threatening, bullying, or excessively toxic behavior is prohibited.

10. No Hate Speech

Discriminatory language and hate speech are not tolerated.

11. No Alternate Accounts

Using alternate accounts to bypass punishments may result in a ban.

12. No Excessive NSFW Discussions

Inappropriate discussions are not allowed.

13. No Troll Tickets

Creating fake or joke support tickets will result in punishment.

14. No Political Discussions

Political debates and discussions are not allowed.

⚠ Punishment System

• Warning

• Timeout / Mute

• Kick

• Permanent Ban


Staff may skip punishment levels depending on the severity of the violation.
